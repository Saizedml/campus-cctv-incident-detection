# v1.0.0 — Curated research release

Portable event/evaluation logic, frozen operating-point configuration, a synthetic review dashboard, CPU tests, aggregate figures and documentation from a research internship at the Cisco–La Trobe Centre for AI and IoT.

Weapon recall is 75.00% at both dashboard tiers. Falls recall is 35.12% and 60.83%. The original criteria were NOT MET for both tasks. Weapons' revised ≤5 criterion was MET under **post-hoc revision (Max, 6 Oct)**; falls' revised criterion was **pre-test** and NOT MET. **GI FAIL:** no passing combined feed count was established. Full confidence intervals, false-alert rates and limitations are in [results](docs/results.md).

Scores are model confidence, not probabilities. This is a research prototype, not deployed. No weights, datasets, annotations or real footage are included. The original project code is MIT; external model and dataset terms remain separate, including the Ultralytics copyleft dependency described in [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Public media are original diagrams, plots of permitted aggregates and clearly labelled synthetic dashboard examples. The complete gated research record remains private. New models or calibration require new evaluation; curation does not change the frozen results.
