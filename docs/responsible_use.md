# Responsible use

This is a research prototype for examining object and posture alerts with a human reviewer. It is not deployed. The system does not recognise faces or identify people. A weapon-like object is not proof of danger, and a person-down notice is not a diagnosis or evidence of intent.

Scores are model confidence, not probabilities. Original criteria were NOT MET for both tasks. The revised weapons criterion was MET only under a **post-hoc revision (Max, 6 Oct)**; the revised falls criterion was **pre-test** and NOT MET. **GI FAIL:** no passing combined feed count was established.

Every operational alert needs human review before action. Do not use this prototype to make autonomous medical, policing, disciplinary or access-control decisions. False alerts, missed incidents, poor site transfer and biased observation can harm people, including by drawing unwarranted suspicion or creating false reassurance.

A future pilot would require lawful footage access, informed governance, access controls, proportionate retention, human escalation procedures and evaluation in its actual environment. Avoid identity tracking, covert surveillance, profiling or use outside the data and model terms. Dataset access is not blanket permission to redistribute images or deploy a model commercially.

Public examples are synthetic. No dataset footage, annotations, checkpoints or deployment credentials are distributed. See [data](data.md), [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [limitations](failure_analysis.md).
